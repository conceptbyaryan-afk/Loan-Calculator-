Days: both the From day and the To day earn interest. Days are counted as real calendar days, but the 31st of a month is not counted (Max 30); February keeps 28/29. Year = 360. Simple: Principal × Rate × Days ÷ 36,000. Compound: interest is added to the principal after every period; each period earns interest on its own days, counted the same way (one compounding period = simple interest for the same days); leftover days at the end are treated the same way.
